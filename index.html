The cue table

Everything is a cue

Available · on REAPER

One ordered list, shared across every department. Numbered, timed and synchronized — the single source of truth from rehearsal to show.

01 · Cue
Number it once

whole.sub numbering keeps strict order. Insert between any two cues without renumbering the show.

02 · Go
Fire on time

Fade, delay and timecode per cue. Trigger by call, by clock, or in sync with playback.

03 · Show
Everyone aligned

Light, audio, video, stage and mechanics read from one shared list — no parallel spreadsheets.

The workspace

Built to be lived in

Table availableDock in development

The cue table is the surface you work on all day. Around it, a bottom dock turns the window into the control room — command line, AI assistant, activity log and history, all docking into the table.

Spreadsheet workflow Available

Edit like a sheet — without leaving the timeline.

  • Cell selection & rectangle ranges across rows and columns
  • Inline edit with Enter / Tab navigation
  • Copy / paste round-trips with Numbers & Excel (TSV)
  • Paste maps by column, even after reordering

The bottom dock In development

Tools dock as tabs and expand into the table.

  • System Monitor — unified activity log of every mutation
  • History — undo steps with multi-step revert
  • Eos Sync — push log to the lighting console
  • Assistant — an AI that acts on cues for you

Module 01

Light

Planned

Lighting works straight from the shared cue table — cue numbers, fades, delays and console commands per cue, pushed to the desk. A dedicated Light edition will add console-aware tooling on top.

From the cue table today

  • Per-cue fade & delay
  • Console command (CMD) field
  • Timecode-driven firing
  • Eos push (in development)

Planned for Light

  • Console-aware cue presets
  • Group / submaster awareness
  • Two-way sync with the desk
  • MA & EOS adapters
