What it does

The cue list is the show

One source of truth

Numbers, names, fades, delays, notes, timecode — every cue lives in one table, in one file. Custom columns for anything your production tracks. Undo everywhere. Nothing hides in a console you can’t read.

Console sync — both directions

Send your cues to ETC Eos or grandMA3. Pull the operator’s edits back. Compare the two worlds side by side and reconcile them cell by cell — nothing is ever overwritten silently.

Audio follows the cues

QGO drives REAPER as its audio timeline: markers mirror your cue list, the playhead follows your selection, and timecode feeds the console. One push — everything lines up.

Verified, not assumed

Every write to a console is read back and confirmed. If a cue didn’t land, QGO says so — precisely which one and why. No green checkmarks over a broken sync.
